A note before you pay anything

Everybody complains about something. A leaky roof, a dead AC, a fence that blew over. People ask their neighbors for help in local groups every single day, and most of those posts get a few guesses and nothing else.

Contractors, meanwhile, spend a lot to find good jobs. When you show up with a real homeowner who needs the work, most will happily pay you a cut. That gap is the whole business.

I'm not going to promise you a number. What you earn depends on you, your area and the contractors you sign. What I will promise is this: I know the method works, so if you do every step and it hasn't paid you back, I'll refund the difference. Every rule is below, before you pay.
