Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-08. Covers leadfarminghq.com and the member portal at leadfarminghq.com/app/.

The short version. We collect what we need to run the Program: your account details, your payment status and the work you log in the CRM. We don't sell your information, we don't use advertising trackers, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Lead Farming Mentoring is run by Alexander Nibarger in Texas ("we", "us"). We are forming Lead Farming Mentoring LLC, which will take our place under this policy once it is formed. Contact us at support@leadfarminghq.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, phone number, password (stored only in scrambled form) and the email of whoever referred you.
  • Payment status: Stripe processes your card. We receive your email, the amount and whether the payment went through. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your work in the portal: the contractors, calls, leads, referral fees, neighbor comment counts, quiz results, level-up requests, refund packages and files you upload as proof.
  • Information about other people that you log: names, businesses and phone numbers of contractors and homeowners. Only enter what you collected honestly and need for your referral business.
  • Messages you send us by email or through "Ask a teacher".

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The portal keeps your login and display settings in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the way any website you visit does.

How we use it

  • To create your account, unlock the Program when you pay, and show your progress and lessons.
  • To review level-up requests and refund packages, which can include contacting contractors or homeowners you named to check your proof.
  • To pay referral credits and handle refunds.
  • To send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password resets, and to answer your questions.
  • To keep the portal secure and to meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

Who can see it

  • You see your own data. Other farmers can't see your contractors or leads.
  • Us and our verifiers (approved teachers who review proof) see your data to run the Program.
  • The person who referred you sees your name and whether you've enrolled, so they know when they've earned their referral credit.
  • Service providers who run parts of the Program for us: Supabase (database, logins and file storage), Stripe (payments), Resend (account emails) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a dispute.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and CRM data while your account is open. After you ask us to delete it, we remove it within 30 days, except payment and refund records we must keep for taxes and accounting (generally up to 7 years).

Your choices

  • You can see and edit most of your data in the portal.
  • Email support@leadfarminghq.com to get a copy of your data, correct it or delete your account. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each person reach the rows they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Age

The Program is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the version date above and tell enrolled farmers about important changes by email or in the portal.
