Common questions

I can't sign in or reset my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. The reset link comes from noreply@leadfarminghq.com, so check your spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

I paid but my account is still locked

It can take a minute. Tap Check again on the Pay screen. If it's still locked after 10 minutes, email us with the email address you paid with.

How do refunds work?

The full rules are in the Terms of Enrollment. In short: hit every effort quota on time, log your work as you go, and send your refund package from the portal by day 97. We refund $500 minus the referral fees contractors paid you by day 90.

I have a billing question or want to dispute a charge

Email us first and we'll sort it out. Card payments are processed by Stripe.

Deleting my account or getting a copy of my data

Email us and we'll handle it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
