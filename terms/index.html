1. Who this agreement is with

This agreement is between you (the "Farmer") and Alex Nibarger, doing business as Lead Farming Mentoring, in Texas ("we", "us"). We are in the process of forming Lead Farming Mentoring LLC, a Texas limited liability company. Once it is formed, the LLC takes our place under this agreement, and nothing else about your enrollment changes.

You must be at least 18 years old and able to make a binding contract to enroll. You agree to this agreement by checking the box when you create your account, and again when you pay.

2. What you get

The Lead Farming Mentoring Program (the "Program") teaches you to connect homeowners with local licensed contractors who pay you a referral fee. When your enrollment is paid, you get access to the member portal at leadfarminghq.com/app/ for at least the 90-day program period and while we keep running the portal after that. The portal includes:

  • Lessons and quizzes, unlocked level by level. You move up a level by passing that level's quiz at 100% and sending proof of your work, which we or a verifier approve.
  • A CRM to log your contractors, calls, leads and referral fees.
  • "Ask a teacher" help from Alex or a teacher we name.
  • Your 90-day tracker, which shows your progress against the refund quotas.

The Program is education and tools. It is not a job, a franchise, a partnership or a business we run for you. You are independent: you choose your hours and methods, and you keep the referral fees contractors pay you.

3. Price and payment

Enrollment is a one-time payment of $500, charged by card through Stripe. There is no subscription and no other required purchase. Your 90 days start on the date your payment clears (the "payment date"). We never see or store your full card number.

4. No earnings promise

We do not promise that you will earn any money. What you earn depends on your effort, your local market, the contractors you work with and things outside anyone's control. Examples of earnings on our site or in lessons are illustrations, not a prediction of your results.

5. Refunds

The $500 enrollment is refundable only under the rules in this section. Outside these rules, it is not refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.

5.2 How much is refunded

If you qualify, the refund is $500 minus the referral fees you earned on jobs closed by day 90. Fees count when the job is closed, whether or not the contractor has paid you yet. If those fees reach $500 or more, no refund is owed. For example: $0 in fees means $500 back; $300 in fees means $200 back.

5.3 How to claim it

  • Send one refund package through the "Submit refund package" button in the portal no later than day 97. Late packages are not accepted.
  • The package must include the proof the portal asks for, such as call-history screenshots, signed agreements or 20% texts, weekly comment screenshots and lead handoff texts.
  • We review the package and tell you the result in the portal. An approved refund goes back to the card you paid with through Stripe.

5.4 Rules for proof

  • The portal CRM is the record. Every contractor, call, lead and fee must be logged in the portal on the day it happened. Entries made in bulk or after the fact do not count.
  • Each piece of evidence counts toward one quota only.
  • We may contact any contractor or homeowner named in your package to check it.
  • Any fake, edited or borrowed item cancels the refund permanently.
  • Bringing in other farmers (recruiting) is never required for a refund and never counts toward it.

5.5 Payment disputes

Please contact us at support@leadfarminghq.com before disputing a charge with your bank. If you dispute a charge, we may pause your portal access while the dispute is open.

6. Introducing a friend

Every paid Farmer may introduce other people to the Program. Introducing anyone is optional and has nothing to do with the refund.

  • You earn $250 when someone you introduced pays their own $500 enrollment and names you as their referrer when they sign up. Nothing is paid for sign-ups that don't pay.
  • If the person you introduced later gets a refund, the $250 is taken back: we deduct it from what we owe you, or you repay it.
  • When you talk about the Program, you may not promise or suggest earnings, call the refund a "guarantee", or say anything that isn't true. Point people to this page for the rules.
  • You are responsible for any taxes on what you earn.

7. How you run your business

  • You connect homeowners with licensed contractors. You do not do contractor work yourself, and you don't hold yourself out as a contractor.
  • You follow the law and the rules of every site you use, including Nextdoor and Facebook. No spam, fake accounts or fake reviews.
  • You make calls and send texts by hand to businesses that expect them. Don't use autodialers or send texts to people who haven't agreed to hear from you.
  • You're responsible for your own taxes, licenses and permits, and for any referral agreement you make with a contractor.
  • Information you log about contractors and homeowners must be collected honestly and used only to run your referral business.

Your login is for you alone; don't share it. The lessons, quizzes, scripts, diagrams and portal belong to us. You may use them to run your own referral business, but you may not copy, resell or publish them. The data you enter in the CRM is yours; our Privacy Policy explains how we handle it.

We may suspend or end your access if you break this agreement, submit fake proof, or misuse the portal or other people's information. If we end your access without cause before day 97, we refund your $500 in full.
